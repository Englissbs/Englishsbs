Section 1: Reading (Passage 1/4)
Traveling in the Digital Age: The way people plan their vacations has changed significantly over the last decade. Previously, travelers relied heavily on travel agencies to book flights and hotels. Today, however, the internet allows individuals to compare prices and read reviews from other travelers in seconds. This accessibility has made travel more affordable for a wider range of people. Nevertheless, the abundance of information online can sometimes be overwhelming for those who prefer a more organized experience.
Section 1: Reading (Passage 2/4)
Environment and Daily Habits: Small changes in our daily routines can have a huge impact on the planet's health. For instance, reducing the use of single-use plastics can prevent tons of waste from entering our oceans. Many cities are now encouraging citizens to use public transportation or bicycles to reduce carbon emissions. While these changes might seem minor, when practiced by millions of people, they contribute to a sustainable future. Education is key to helping people understand that their individual choices really do matter in the long run.
Section 1: Reading (Passage 3/4)
Social Media and Communication: The rise of social media platforms has redefined how we maintain our relationships. We can now stay connected with friends and family across the globe in real-time. This has made the world feel like a much smaller place. On the other hand, some experts worry that digital communication is replacing deep, face-to-face interactions. It is common to see groups of people in restaurants looking at their phones instead of talking to each other. Finding a balance between the digital and physical worlds is essential.
Section 1: Reading (Passage 4/4)
Remote Work Challenges: Working from home became a necessity for many during the recent global health crisis. While many employees enjoy the flexibility and the lack of a commute, others struggle with feelings of isolation. Without the structure of a traditional office, it can be difficult to separate work life from personal time. Some people find themselves working longer hours than they did in the office. To be successful, remote workers need to establish a clear schedule and a dedicated workspace to maintain their productivity and mental health.
